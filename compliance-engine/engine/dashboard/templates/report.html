How this was measured

Each check carries a weight from 1 to 10. The score is the weight earned divided by the weight of the checks that apply to this site, so nothing is counted against you that is not relevant — a site with no video is not marked down for missing captions. Checks that need a person, and checks the tools could not decide, are excluded from the percentage entirely and listed above instead. A percentage is a measurement of automated conformance, not a statement that a website is legally compliant. No one can promise that, and we don't.
