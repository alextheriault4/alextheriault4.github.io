What we collect, and where it comes from

  • Business contact details — a business name, website, category, town and a published business email address, taken from public business directories, OpenStreetMap, and the contact page of the business's own website. We do not buy lists and we do not collect personal addresses.
  • A copy of public pages from the website, kept so we can show exactly what was wrong and, if hired, produce a corrected version. Deleted automatically after {{ settings.legal.snapshot_retention_days }} days.
  • Email correspondence with us, and whether a message was delivered or opted out of.
  • For customers, the payment record from our payment processor (we never see or store card numbers) and any site access credentials you choose to give us, encrypted and deleted once the work is delivered.

Why

To offer a service to the business, to carry out that service if hired, and to keep proof that we honoured opt-outs. We do not sell, rent or share this data with anyone, and we do not use it to train models.

Your choices

  • Stop the email — reply with the word "unsubscribe", or use the link in any message. It takes effect immediately and is permanent.
  • Delete everything — reply with the word "delete", use the erase link in any message, or email {{ company.support_email }}. We erase the page copies, the scan results and the message contents. We keep only the suppressed address itself, because that is what stops us contacting you again.
  • Keep the crawler away — see our crawler page.

Scope

We contact businesses in the United States only.
