What we sell

We scan small-business websites for accessibility problems (measured against WCAG 2.1/2.2 AA) and for whether search engines and AI assistants can read the business's information, and we fix the issues we report, for a flat fee. We are not a law firm and provide no legal advice. We do not sell an accessibility “overlay” or widget, and we do not offer certification. Any dollar figures in our emails or reports are estimates from published sources, clearly labelled as such, and are not predictions about your business.

Our email

We send a small number of one-to-one business emails to US businesses whose websites we have scanned, offering this service. Reply with “unsubscribe” and you will not hear from us again; reply with “delete” and we will erase what we hold about you.
