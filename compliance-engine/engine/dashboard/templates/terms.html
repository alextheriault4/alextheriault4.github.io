What we sell

We scan small-business websites for accessibility problems (measured against WCAG 2.1/2.2 AA) and for whether search engines and AI assistants can read the business's information, and we fix the issues we report, for a flat fee. Each engagement is governed by the service agreement shown before payment.

The monthly plan

The monthly plan rechecks your site against our checklist every month and fixes what has slipped or what is new. The checklist itself is versioned (currently edition {{ checklist_version }}) and kept current: when the accessibility guidelines change, or search engines and AI assistants change what they read, the new checks are added and your site is measured against them at the next monthly check, at no extra charge. You can cancel at any time, in one click, with no notice period and no cancellation fee.

What we do not sell

We are not a law firm and provide no legal advice. We do not sell an accessibility “overlay” or widget, and we do not offer certification. No one can guarantee that a website will not attract an accessibility complaint or lawsuit, and we do not claim to. Any dollar figures in our emails or reports are estimates from published sources, clearly labelled as such, and are not predictions about your business.

Our email

We send a small number of one-to-one business emails to US businesses whose websites we have scanned, offering this service. Every message identifies us, gives our postal address, and includes a working opt-out that we honour immediately and permanently. Reply with “unsubscribe” and you will not hear from us again; reply with “delete” and we will erase what we hold about you.
