About our crawler

If you found this page from your server logs, this explains what visited your site.

What it is

ComplianceEngineBot is operated by {{ company.legal_name }}. It reads a small number of public pages on a website to check them for accessibility problems (against the WCAG 2.1 AA guidelines) and for whether search engines and AI assistants can read the business's details. We use the result to offer a service to the site's owner.

What it does

  • Requests robots.txt first and obeys it, including Crawl-delay.
  • Reads at most a handful of pages per site, several seconds apart.
  • Sends only ordinary GET requests for pages that are already public.
  • Never attempts to log in, submit a form, or request an admin, account, checkout or configuration path.
  • Never tries passwords, never probes for vulnerabilities, never follows links it was asked not to.

How to stop it

Add this to your robots.txt and it will not come back:

User-agent: ComplianceEngineBot
Disallow: /

Or email {{ company.support_email }} and we will add your domain to our permanent do-not-contact list and delete anything we hold about your site. Either way we will not email you.
